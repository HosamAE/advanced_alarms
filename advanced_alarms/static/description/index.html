Advanced Alarms & Time Management

A complete, elegant, and fully integrated productivity suite. Manage alarms, stopwatches, timers, and world clocks seamlessly within Odoo.

Productivity Time Management RTL / LTR

Transform How You Manage Time

Advanced Alarms brings a professional time management system directly into your interface. No external tools, no integrations needed. From critical alerts to multi-lap stopwatches, everything lives in a beautifully designed systray widget with real-time notifications.

Smart Alarms

Set one-time or recurring alarms with priority levels (Critical, High, Normal, Low). Critical alarms take audio-visual precedence.

Countdown Timers

Run multiple timers simultaneously with a clean HH:MM:SS picker. Timers auto-dismiss upon completion.

Multi-lap Stopwatches

Keep track of duration precisely with multi-lap stopwatches. Sessions persist beautifully in the background.

World Clocks

View live local times for major cities worldwide, fully integrated into the top bar with automatic DST adjustment.

Custom Sounds

Choose from 5 built-in modern notification sounds, or easily upload your own MP3/OGG audio files per alarm.

Responsive & RTL

Perfectly optimized for mobile screens and completely supports bidirectional text (RTL/LTR) natively.
