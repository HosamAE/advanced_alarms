The True Power of Advanced Alarms

Designed for complex business operations, not just simple reminders.

Shift & Cycle Engine

Configure "3 days on, 4 days off" shifts, or complex 30-day repeating cycles. Built natively for shift workers, hospitals, and factories.

Team Broadcasting

Target alarms to specific Security Groups. Alert the whole warehouse team or the sales department instantly.

Deep Record Integration

Alarms are directly linked to Odoo models. Click an alarm notification to instantly open the related Invoice, Lead, or Task.

Real-time WebSockets

Zero-refresh notifications. Utilizing Odoo's native bus.bus, alarms ring directly in the browser exactly on time.

Intraday Repeating

Set alarms that ring every 4 hours or every 30 minutes. Perfect for medication reminders or hourly checkups.

Developer API

Easily trigger alarms from your custom modules using create_alarm_from_code().
